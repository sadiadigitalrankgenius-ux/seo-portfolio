The problem

The site had almost 500 blog posts with no clear topic structure. One list post had 420,450 impressions but only a 0.32% CTR at position 26.6, so most of its visibility gave no clicks.

What I did
  • Set the five semantic fundamentals: source context, central entity, central search intent, core section and outer section.
  • Read the sitemaps of 8 competitors, including The Shelf, Viral Nation, Upfluence and Sprout Social, and logged every topic they cover.
  • Built a research sheet of entities, attributes, predicates, lexical semantics, taxonomy, ontology and buyer perspectives.
  • Gave every query in the map a section (root, core or outer) and an action: write, rewrite, merge or redirect.
Content delivered
  • Semantic content briefs for the home page and 7 corporate pages, including About, Services, Locations and Case Studies.
  • Finalized briefs and articles for 9 topical map nodes, such as influencer marketing metrics, Instagram and Twitch influencer marketing, and micro vs macro influencers.
  • A plan for 34 location pages and 68 influencer session pages, each marked rewrite, configure or keep.

How I Work

Every project follows the same steps, so results do not depend on luck.

Read the data

I start with Search Console, Ahrefs and a full crawl of the live site.

Study the niche

I map the persona, the target country and the real competitors on Google.

List the faults

I find what blocks ranking and clicks, then sort fixes by impact.

Build the topical map

I use the five semantic fundamentals and keep only services the site sells.

Publish and track

My team and I publish on a daily plan and check the numbers each week.
